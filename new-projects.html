Custom software, built around how you already work

Somewhere in your company there's a process that runs the place, and it lives in spreadsheets and a handful of tools that don't talk to each other. We build the single system that process deserved from the start.

What we build

Internal tools

Operations systems for scheduling, quoting, inventory, job tracking, and the handoffs between departments that email was never meant to carry.

Customer portals

A place where your clients log in to see status, documents, and invoices for themselves, instead of asking your team by phone and email.

Custom CRMs

Pipeline, accounts, and follow-up shaped around how your sales process actually runs, with the fields your team needs and none of the ones it doesn't.

Integrations and automation

Connections between the systems you're keeping, like accounting or e-commerce, so data is entered once and flows where it's needed.

How a project runs

  1. Scope

    We sit with the people who do the work and map the process as they describe it, including the exceptions nobody ever wrote down. You get a written scope: what the system covers, who uses it, what data moves in, and what it will cost.

  2. Design

    Before any code, you see the screens and the data model on paper. It's much cheaper to move a column in a mockup than in a live system, so this is where opinions are welcome and changes are free.

  3. Build in increments

    Working software shows up within the first weeks, in pieces your team can click through and react to. What you learn from each piece feeds the next one, so the plan can bend before anything is expensive to change.

  4. Launch

    We load your existing data from the spreadsheets and tools being retired, train your team on the real system, and switch on. Old tools get turned off deliberately, not abandoned half-alive.

  5. Improve

    The first weeks of real use always teach something. We stay on to refine, support, and extend the system as the business changes, at whatever pace suits you.

Ownership, spelled out

"You own it" is the point of working with us, so here is what it means in practice.

  • The code is yours. Source code, documentation, and data belong to you, not to us and not to a platform.
  • No per-seat fees. Hiring your fifty-first employee doesn't raise the price of your own software.
  • No forced changes. Nothing is redesigned, deprecated, or re-priced over your head.
  • No hostage clause. The system is documented so another developer could take it on. We'd rather earn the next project than trap you into it.

Describe the process. We'll describe the system.

A free consultation is enough to sketch what the right system looks like and roughly what it takes to build. Some processes should stay a spreadsheet, and when that's the honest answer we'll say so.
