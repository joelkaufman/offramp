About Offramp Software

We started Offramp because we kept watching good companies rent software that didn't fit, and pay more for it every year. The name is the promise: a safe way off the platform treadmill, onto something you own.

How we work

These habits are what we've found keeps projects calm and keeps systems alive years after launch.

  • Plain language. Plans and progress reports you can read without a translator.
  • Working software early. You judge the real thing as it takes shape instead of a slide deck about it.
  • Boring migrations. Every move is rehearsed first and reversible after. Drama is a planning failure.
  • Built to be left. Documented systems any competent developer could take over. That's what ownership means.
